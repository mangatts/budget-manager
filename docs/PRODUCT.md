# Product

## The job

"Tell me what my credit cards are at, what I earn after my fixed bills, and what I should keep paying."

That's the whole app. It replaced the earlier, bigger Budget Manager (tag `v1-full-budget-manager`) because tracking every purchase was more work than the question needed.

## What you enter

| Once (Setup) | Whenever you like (Check-in) |
|---|---|
| Income: amount and how often (weekly, every 2 weeks, twice a month, monthly) | A date |
| Fixed expenses: rent, phone, car, insurance, groceries and gas, with an optional due day | Each card's balance that day |
| Cards: nickname, APR, minimum payment, optional credit limit | |

**People:** more than one person can use the app on the same device (More → People). Each has their own Setup, check-ins and plan, and the screens show whose money you're looking at. It's for convenience, not privacy: anyone with the device can switch.

## What Home tells you

1. **Each month:** money coming in − fixed expenses − card minimums = what's left for paying down cards (or how much you're short).
2. **Pay this month:** every card gets its minimum. Everything left goes to the card with the **highest interest rate** (the "avalanche" method costs the least interest). That card is marked "Focus here".
3. **Keep paying every month:** your fixed expenses, ordered by due day.
4. **Your card debt:** the total, the change since your previous check-in, the share of your credit limits you're using (under 30% is best for your credit score), and a debt-free estimate if you stick to the plan.
5. **Past check-ins:** tap one to fix or delete it.

## Principles

- **Simple:** 3 screens plus More. Check-in takes about 30 seconds.
- **Honest math:** integer cents, interest charged monthly in the payoff estimate, and "at this rate the balances won't go down" when that's the truth.
- **Private:** on-device only, no bank connection, no card numbers. Encrypted backups to move between devices.
- **For every age:** large readable type, three text sizes, light and dark themes, plain words, Undo instead of "Are you sure?".
